# Senior Educator

Senior Educators are experienced educators with leadership responsibilities for teaching and learning activities. 

## Example Activities:

-   Taught Module Lead (Organise teaching sessions, recruit and distribute work between the markers)
-   Attendance at teaching committees and exam boards
-   Mentoring of other Educators
-   Lead on the development of new training courses and modules
-   Introduce new, or substantial revision of old modules/courses in response to student feedback or to update content and/or delivery modes
-   Contribute to development of local education strategy including impact and evaluation
-   Apply for grants to support innovation in teaching
-   Explore/implement innovative teaching practices that impact positively on diverse group
-   Member of a [Carpentries Lesson Programme Governance Committee](https://carpentries.org/community/lesson_program_governors/) or equivalent
