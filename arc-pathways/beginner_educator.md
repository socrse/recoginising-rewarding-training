# Beginner Educator

## Teaching and Instructing
**Example Activities:**

-   Workshop Helper (being proactively on a class observing the learners progress and alerting the instructor if needed)
-   Workshop Instructor (teach a session or part of it while observed by a more experienced educator)
-   Support Development of Course Materials (writing exercises, reviewing material, contributing external lessons such as Carpentries')
-   Act as a Teaching Assistant for taught modules (support on office hours, review of assignment, marking assistance)

**Indicators of Impact:**

-   Mainly positive participant feedback for courses delivered or co-delivered
-   AdvanceHE Associate Fellow
-   Associate CMALT

**CPD Opportunities:** 

-   UCL ARENA workshops
-   Digital Education courses (e.g., accessibility, moodle, mentimeter, ...)
-   Participating at [ARC/DSD Peer Obs Scheme](../Peer-Observation-scheme.md)
-   [ARC Teach & Code Socials](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/advanced-research-computing/education) and education themed conferences
-   [Turing Institute Data Science and AI Educators' Programme](https://www.turing.ac.uk/data-science-and-ai-educators-programme)
-   [Carpentries Instructor Training](https://carpentries.github.io/instructor-training/training_calendar.html)
