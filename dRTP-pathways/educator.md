# Educator

Educators are a little more experienced, and are more involved in Education activities and seek (or have sought) accreditation.
They are taking more responsibility in teaching and learning environments.

## Example Activities:

-   Workshop Instructor as a Certified Carpentries Instructor
-   Workshop Organiser
-   Increased responsibility as a Teaching Assistant for taught modules (office hours with individual students or groups)
-   Co-deliver taught modules (discuss planning, lead sessions, assessment design)
-   Development and review of Course Materials (designing content, plan a class)
-   Investigate new teaching tools
-   Be actively engaged with relevant training and education related communities
-   Be a maintainer for a Carpentries lesson
-   Being a member of an education related special interest group
-   Participating in projects that contribute to improving education for students
-   Proactively request feedback and reflect on teaching practice

## Continuing Professional Development Opportunities:

-   Present at an education focused event
-   Attend an education conference
-   PGCert Higher Education
-   [PGCert Learning and Teaching in Higher Education](https://www.london.ac.uk/study/courses/postgraduate/pgcert-learning-teaching-higher-education)
-   [AdvanceHE Fellowship](https://advance-he.ac.uk/fellowship/)
-   [CMALT](https://www.alt.ac.uk/cmalt-accreditation-framework)
-   Engage in the [scholarship of teaching and learning (SOTL)](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/bilt/our-work-and-who-we-are/scholarship-of-teaching-and-learning/)
-   [City and Guilds Learning and Development (6318)](https://www.cityandguilds.com/qualifications-and-apprenticeships/learning/training-and-development/6318-learning-and-development#tab=documents)
