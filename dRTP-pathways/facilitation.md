# Teaching and Training Facilitation

In addition to learning design and delivery activities, there are a number of key facilitation activities required to enable the delivery and scheduling of education and activities. These tasks may be undertaken by those in dRTP roles, or other colleagues.

These activities may include, but are not limited:
- recruitment of instructors and helpers
- scheduling of workshops
- room bookings
- promotion of workshops
- creation of workshop websites
- creation of collaborative notes documents
- managing workshop attendance e.g. adding event to a learning platform or creating an event on a ticketing system
- acting as a Code of Conduct point of contact
- organising catering
- scheduling and facilitating retrospectives
