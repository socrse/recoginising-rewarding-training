# Associate Educator

Associate Educators may be those who have limited time to contribute to teaching and training activity, or those new to teaching and training. 

## Example Activities:
-   Workshop Helper (being proactively in a class observing the learners progress and alerting the instructor if needed)
-   Workshop Instructor (teach a session or part of it while supported by a more experienced educator)
-   Support Development of Course Materials (writing exercises, reviewing material, contributing external lessons such as Carpentries')
-   Act as a Teaching Assistant for taught modules (support on office hours, review of assignment, marking assistance)
-   Support the maintenance and development of learning materials
-   Assist in supporting platforms used for teaching and learning activities
