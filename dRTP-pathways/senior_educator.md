# Senior Educator

Pay scale points: 39 to 44

## Teaching and Instructing

**Example Activities:**

-   Taught Module Lead (Organise teaching sessions, recruit and distribute work between the markers)
-   Attendance at teaching committees and exam boards
-   Carpentries Instructor Trainer
-   Mentoring of other Educators
-   Lead on the development of new training courses and modules
-   Contribute to development of [ARC Education Strategy](../Strategy-Development.md)
-   Member of relevant SIGs and CoPs at UCL or beyond (RSE, Carpentries, ...)

**Indicators of Impact:**

-   Introduction of new, or substantial revision of old modules/courses in response to student feedback or to update content and/or delivery modes
-   Recipient of grants to support innovation in teaching
-   Evidence of innovative teaching practices that impact positively on diverse groups
-   Reviewer for education-focused journal, book series or [software](https://jose.theoj.org/about)
-   Authorship/delivery of conference presentations, book and book chapters, peer-reviewed publications and citations focused on teaching and learning
-   AdvanceHE Senior Fellow
-   Senior CMALT
-   [SEDA Fellowship](https://www.seda.ac.uk/professional-development-opportunities/fellowships-scheme/fellowship/)

**CPD Opportunities:** 

-   UCL ARENA workshops
-   ARC/DSD Peer Obs Scheme
-   Presenting at ARC Teach & Code Socials and education themed conferences
-   [Carpentries Instructor Trainer Training](https://docs.carpentries.org/topic_folders/instructor_training/trainers_training.html)
